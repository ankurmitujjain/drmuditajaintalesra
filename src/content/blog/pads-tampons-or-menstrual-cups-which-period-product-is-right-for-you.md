## Making an Informed Menstrual Health Choice

Menstrual hygiene is fundamental to women's reproductive health and daily confidence. With evolving products available, understanding the pros, cons, and hygiene guidelines of each option helps you choose what works best for your body.

---

## Detailed Comparison Table

| Product | Mechanism | Wear Time | Reusability | Ideal For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sanitary Pads** | External absorbent pad adhered to underwear | Change every 4–6 hrs | Single-use disposable | Beginners, postpartum recovery, light sleep |
| **Tampons** | Internal absorbent cotton cylinder with retrieval string | Change every 4–8 hrs | Single-use disposable | Swimmers, athletes, active workouts |
| **Menstrual Cups** | Internal medical-grade silicone bell that collects flow | Empty every 8–12 hrs | Reusable for up to 5–10 years | Eco-conscious women, travelers, heavy flow |

---

## Key Hygiene Rules Every Woman Must Practice

- Always wash hands thoroughly before and after inserting or changing internal products.
- Sterilize reusable menstrual cups in boiling water for 5 to 7 minutes before the start of each menstrual cycle.
- Never ignore persistent vaginal itching, foul odor, or unusual discharge—consult Dr. Mudita Jain Talesra promptly.
