## A Critical Gynecological Condition Demanding Early Vigilance

In a healthy pregnancy, the fertilized egg travels through the fallopian tube to implant inside the thick, vascular lining of the uterus. When implantation occurs elsewhere, it is termed an **ectopic pregnancy**.

Early medical identification prevents emergency tubal rupture, enabling conservative medication (methotrexate) or fertility-preserving laparoscopic surgery.

---

## Cardinal Symptoms to Watch For

- **Unilateral Pelvic Pain:** Sharp, stabbing, or cramp-like discomfort localized to one side of the lower abdomen.
- **Abnormal Vaginal Spotting:** Dark brown or prune-colored bleeding distinct from a normal menstrual period.
- **Positive Pregnancy Test:** Accompanied by pelvic discomfort or abnormal hCG rise.
- **Shoulder Tip Pain:** A classic warning sign indicating blood pooling under the diaphragm irritating the phrenic nerve.

---

## Management Modalities

1. **Medical Management (Methotrexate):** Administered in early, unruptured cases with low beta-hCG levels (<5,000 mIU/mL) and no visible fetal cardiac activity.
2. **Minimally Invasive Laparoscopy:** Keyhole surgical removal of the ectopic gestation (salpingostomy or salpingectomy) while preserving pelvic organs.
