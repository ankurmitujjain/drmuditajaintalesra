## The Fourth Trimester: Nurturing the New Mother

While newborn care naturally takes center stage after delivery, maternal healing is equally vital. Your body has undergone 9 months of intense anatomical and hormonal restructuring that requires adequate time, rest, and nutritional replenishment to heal.

---

## Physical Healing Checklist

1. **Incision & Perineal Care:**
   - Keep episiotomy or cesarean incision sites clean and dry. Warm sitz baths soothe perineal tenderness.
2. **Uterine Involution & Cramps:**
   - The uterus contracts back to its pre-pregnancy size over 6 weeks. Cramps (afterpains) are common during breastfeeding due to natural oxytocin release.
3. **Pelvic Floor Strengthening:**
   - Begin gentle Kegel exercises once approved by your doctor to rebuild bladder support and prevent stress urinary incontinence.
4. **Hydration & Lactation Nutrition:**
   - Consume adequate protein, iron, calcium, and fluids to support breast milk synthesis and maternal tissue repair.
