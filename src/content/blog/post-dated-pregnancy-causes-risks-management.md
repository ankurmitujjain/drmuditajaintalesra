## Navigating the Days Beyond Your Due Date

Reaching your 40-week Estimated Date of Delivery (EDD) without labor signs can feel anxious. In reality, only about 5% of babies arrive on their exact calculated due date; most arrive safely between weeks 38 and 41.

However, continuing beyond 41 weeks requires structured obstetric vigilance to guarantee fetal safety.

---

## Clinical Monitoring Protocol After 40 Weeks

- **Non-Stress Test (NST / CTG):** Electronic fetal heart rate monitoring evaluating cardiac response to fetal movements.
- **Amniotic Fluid Index (AFI):** Ultrasound measurement to verify adequate protective fluid surrounding the baby.
- **Biophysical Profile (BPP):** Combined ultrasound assessment of fetal breathing, body movements, muscle tone, and fluid volume.
- **Umbilical Artery Doppler:** Measures placental blood flow resistance.
