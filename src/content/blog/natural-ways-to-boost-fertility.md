## Preparing Your Body for a Healthy Pregnancy

Conception is a delicate biological process governed by hormonal balance, nutritional sufficiency, and emotional well-being. Simple, evidence-backed lifestyle choices can significantly optimize reproductive outcomes for both women and men.

**Dr. Mudita Jain Talesra (MS, FICOG, FMFI)** shares essential clinical guidance for couples planning pregnancy in Central India.

---

## 5 Clinically Proven Ways to Optimize Conception

1. **Pinpoint Your Fertile Window:**
   - In a 28-day cycle, ovulation typically occurs around day 14. Track cervical mucus changes (clear, slippery egg-white consistency) or use home urine LH ovulation test kits.
2. **Embrace an Antioxidant-Dense Diet:**
   - Include leafy greens, legumes, nuts, seeds, and colorful fruits rich in folate, vitamin E, zinc, and selenium to protect egg and sperm DNA integrity.
3. **Start Preconceptional Folic Acid (5 mg/day):**
   - Supplementing folic acid at least 2 to 3 months prior to conception reduces the incidence of neural tube defects (spina bifida) by over 70%.
4. **Regulate Sleep and Cortisol:**
   - Chronic sleep deprivation elevates cortisol and prolactin, disrupting regular gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) pulses necessary for monthly ovulation.
5. **Male Partner Health:**
   - Avoid tight clothing, hot saunas, and laptop placement on the lap, as scrotal temperature elevation impairs sperm count and motility.
