## A Compassionate, Evidence-Driven Path to Parenthood

Conceiving a child can sometimes take unexpected time. Approximately 15% of couples experience fertility challenges. Modern reproductive medicine offers clear diagnostic pathways that resolve barriers to conception without unnecessary or invasive treatments.

## Step-by-Step Diagnostic Framework

1. **Ovarian Reserve & Hormonal Evaluation:**
   - Serum Anti-Müllerian Hormone (AMH) to assess remaining follicular reserve.
   - Day-2/3 hormonal profile: FSH, LH, Estradiol, Prolactin, and TSH.
   - Baseline Transvaginal Ultrasound (TVS) for Antral Follicle Count (AFC).
2. **Fallopian Tube Patency Assessment:**
   - Hysterosalpingography (HSG) or Sono-salpingography to confirm open fallopian tubes.
   - Diagnostic laparoscopy and chromopertubation when pelvic adhesions or endometriosis are suspected.
3. **Male Partner Semen Analysis:**
   - Evaluates sperm count, motility, and morphology following WHO standards.
4. **Ovulatory Tracking & Timed Intercourse:**
   - Serial ultrasound follicular monitoring paired with oral ovulogens (letrozole / clomiphene).
5. **Intrauterine Insemination (IUI):**
   - Ideal for mild male factor subfertility, unexplained infertility, or hostile cervical mucus.
