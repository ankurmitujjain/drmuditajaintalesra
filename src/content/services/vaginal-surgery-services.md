## Scar-Free Pelvic Floor Reconstruction & Uterine Surgeries

Pelvic organ prolapse occurs when the supportive muscles and fascial tissues of the pelvic floor become stretched or weakened, allowing the uterus, bladder (cystocele), or bowel (rectocele) to descend into or through the vaginal canal.

**Dr. Mudita Jain Talesra (MS, FMAS, FICOG)** possesses extensive expertise in reconstructive vaginal surgery, allowing patients to regain comfort, bladder control, and quality of life without abdominal incisions.

## Surgical Procedures Performed

- **Anterior & Posterior Colporrhaphy:** Tightening and repairing weakened vaginal walls to correct bladder bulge (cystocele) and rectal bulge (rectocele).
- **Vaginal Hysterectomy with Pelvic Floor Repair:** Removal of prolapsed uterus combined with anatomical repair of supporting ligaments.
- **Non-Descent Vaginal Hysterectomy (NDVH):** Safe removal of non-prolapsed uterus through the vaginal route for benign conditions like fibroids or dysfunctional bleeding.
- **Sacrospinous Ligament Fixation:** Anchoring the vaginal vault to deep pelvic ligaments to prevent recurrent apex prolapse.
