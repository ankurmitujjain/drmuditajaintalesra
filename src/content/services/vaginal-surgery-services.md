## True Natural-Orifice Surgery: No Abdominal Scars

While modern laparoscopic keyhole surgery has significantly reduced the invasiveness of abdominal operations, **vaginal surgery** represents the ultimate form of scarless, natural-orifice minimally invasive gynecological surgery. By operating entirely through the natural vaginal opening, the patient experiences **zero abdominal incisions**, minimal postoperative discomfort, and rapid convalescence.

At **Mudita Women Health Care Center** in Bhopal, **Dr. Mudita Jain Talesra** possesses specialized surgical expertise in operative vaginal reconstructions and **Non-Descent Vaginal Hysterectomy (NDVH)**, developed over 17 years of surgical practice.

## Key Vaginal Surgical Procedures

### 1. Non-Descent Vaginal Hysterectomy (NDVH)
Historically, when a diseased uterus was not prolapsed, surgeons automatically resorted to open abdominal surgery. With specialized morcellation and debulking skills (bisection, myomectomy, wedge resection), Dr. Mudita removes moderately enlarged fibroid or adenomyotic uteri safely via the vaginal route:
- **No Abdominal Scars:** True scarless surgery.
- **Lower Complication Rates:** Decreased risk of bowel injury, wound infection, and incisional hernia compared to abdominal surgery.

### 2. Pelvic Organ Prolapse Repair (Cystocele & Rectocele)
Pelvic organ prolapse (बच्चेदानी का बाहर आना) occurs when the pelvic floor muscles, cardinal-uterosacral ligaments, and endopelvic fascia weaken:
- **Anterior Colporrhaphy:** Tightens the pubocervical fascia supporting the bladder, resolving bladder protrusion (cystocele) and urinary frequency.
- **Posterior Colpoperineorrhaphy:** Repairs the rectovaginal septum supporting the rectum (rectocele), resolving painful defecation and pelvic heaviness.
- **Vaginal Hysterectomy with Pelvic Floor Repair:** For advanced third- or fourth-degree uterovaginal prolapse, combined with apical vault suspension (such as sacrospinous ligament fixation) to prevent recurrence.
