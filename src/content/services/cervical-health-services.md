## Cervical Cancer is 100% Preventable: Protect Your Health

In India, cervical cancer accounts for a significant portion of cancer cases in women. Yet, because cervical cell changes develop slowly over 5 to 15 years following persistent Human Papillomavirus (HPV) infection, regular screening and vaccination eliminate over 90% of cervical cancer risk.

As a **National Master Trainer for HPV Vaccination**, **Dr. Mudita Jain Talesra** has educated hundreds of clinicians and advocated for women\'s preventive oncology across Madhya Pradesh.

## Essential Screening Modalities

1. **Liquid-Based Cytology (LBC) Pap Smear:**
   - Collects superficial cervical cells to detect atypical or precancerous dysplasia (CIN-I, CIN-II, CIN-III) years before malignancy develops.
2. **High-Risk HPV DNA Testing:**
   - Detects oncogenic viral strains, particularly HPV 16 and 18, responsible for over 70% of invasive cervical cancers.
3. **Colposcopy & Targeted Biopsy:**
   - Magnified visual examination of the cervix with specialized staining (acetic acid / Lugol\'s iodine) to pinpoint suspicious transformation zones for precise biopsy.
4. **HPV Vaccination:**
   - 9-valent and 4-valent vaccines available for young girls, adolescents, and adult women up to age 45.
