### Positioning & Audience
"The GynaeSurgeon Vault" is tailored for medical professionals, postgraduate OBG residents, and laparoscopic trainees across India.

### Monetization Eligibility (YPP 2025/2026 Standards)
1. **Full Monetization (AdSense + Brand Collaborations):**

2. **Early Tier Fan-Funding (Channel Memberships & Super Thanks):**
   - 500 Subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and 3,000 watch hours (or 3M Shorts views).
3. **Monetization Paths Beyond AdSense:**
   - **Resident Exam Prep Series:** High watch-hour retention deep-dives into operative vaginal delivery, breech extraction, and laparoscopic port placements.
   - **Channel Memberships:** Tiered memberships offering unedited surgical case footage and monthly resident viva prep sessions.
   - **Medical Equipment & Healthcare Sponsorships:** Ethical collaborations with surgical instrument manufacturers, sutures, and ultrasound technologies strictly compliant with NMC regulations.

### Surgical Video Policy & Discretion
Operative surgical footage must adhere to YouTube's graphic content guidelines:
- Apply mild clinical color desaturation or blur on active blood fields where appropriate.
- Ensure thumbnails feature anatomical diagrams rather than raw surgical cuts to avoid age-restriction algorithms.
- Maintain absolute patient anonymity and written consent for surgical educational recording.
